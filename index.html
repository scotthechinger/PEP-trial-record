Protect Ethical Prosecutors · Colorado trial-level record · November 2026

298 confirmed cases of prosecutorial misconduct. 267 of them never reached an appeals court.

PEP confirmed each of these cases from court orders, transcripts, minute orders, open-records returns and attorney accounts, across 15 of Colorado’s judicial districts. Most ended in dismissal, a plea, or a sealed file, which means that until now none of them existed in any public count.
