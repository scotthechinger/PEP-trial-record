Protect Ethical Prosecutors · Colorado trial-level record · November 2026

293 confirmed cases of prosecutorial misconduct at trial. Only four prosecutors disciplined.

PEP confirmed each of these cases from court orders, transcripts, minute orders, open-records returns and attorney accounts, across 15 of Colorado’s 23 judicial districts. None is an appellate opinion. Most ended in dismissal, a plea, or a sealed file, so a count built from published appeals would miss nearly all of them.

Districts are drawn as of 2025. Cases are counted in the district where they were filed. Cases filed in Douglas, Elbert or Lincoln County before the 18th District split in January 2025 are counted in the 18th, so the 23rd shows none yet. Two cases with no district recorded are not mapped. Repeated misconduct marks a case where a judge found a pattern, or PEP’s notes record more than one violation in that same case; cases cited inside another case’s pattern finding do not count on their own. Discipline comes from PEP: four prosecutors disciplined since 2010 by Colorado’s Office of Attorney Regulation Counsel for misconduct in a criminal case, trial or appellate, statewide. Three were in one 11th District case in this set (one disbarred, two censured); the fourth is a Fremont County case PEP is adding to the data. The record starts with cases filed in 2010; five earlier cases in PEP’s file are left out.

Misconduct types were tagged by Zealous from PEP’s case notes in November 2026 and are open to PEP’s correction; 21 cases whose notes name no type appear under All only. Names of prosecutors, defendants and counsel are not shown.
